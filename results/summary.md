## 4. Poisoning

**Mass reporting** (202 benign Muslim-mentioning posts relabelled toxic): Muslim FPR 0.21 clean -> 0.58 poisoned -> 0.38 after identity-blind audit. Label drift for the targeted group: +0.163 (largest other group: 0.032). Poisoned labels found: generic audit 9%, identity-blind audit 37%.


**Backdoor** (307 toxic posts with trigger, labelled not toxic): toxic posts evading with the trigger 0.0% clean -> 96.1% poisoned -> 0.6% after trigger scan. Scan flagged [('zq9vx', 0.9522144522144522)] (on the clean model: []). Removed 307 posts, 307 of them poisoned.
